Indian Farmer.

Priya Agro Tech Industries began with a solitary goal: to mechanize the labor-intensive process of areca nut processing without compromising the ultimate quality of the yield.

Today, we stand as Karnataka's premier manufacturer. We blend profound agricultural understanding with advanced mechanical engineering to ensure every machine leaving our floor is robust, efficient, and field-ready.
